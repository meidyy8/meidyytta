The Food

Tempoyak

Tempoyak is a traditional Jambi dish made from fermented durian. The durian flesh is mixed with salt and left to ferment for a few days, creating a sour and rich flavor. Tempoyak is commonly used as a condiment or cooked into spicy dishes, often paired with fish such as patin (Pangasius fish). It is considered a signature food of Jambi and a must-try for visitors who want to experience local culinary culture.
