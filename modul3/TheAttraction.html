The Attraction

Danau Kaco

Danau Kaco is a beautiful natural lake located in Kerinci Seblat National Park, Jambi Province, Indonesia. The lake is famous for its incredibly clear and blue-colored water that looks like glass. Danau Kaco is surrounded by tropical rainforest and is one of the popular tourist destinations in Jambi, especially for nature lovers and hikers. Visitors usually reach the lake by trekking through the forest from Lempur Village.
