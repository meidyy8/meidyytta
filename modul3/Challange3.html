The City

Jambi is the capital of Jambi Province, located on the island of Sumatra, Indonesia. The city is the center of government, commerce, and culture in the province. Jambi City boasts a blend of modern areas and well-preserved Malay culture.

What?

Jambi is a city on the island of Sumatra.

Where?

Jambi Province, Sumatra Island, Indonesia.

How?

You can reach Jambi through Sultan Thaha Airport which connects the city with major cities in Indonesia.
